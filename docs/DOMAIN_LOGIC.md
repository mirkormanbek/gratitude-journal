# Domain logic

## Daily journal
Core concepts historically included:
- gratitude to Allah;
- gratitude to self;
- lesson/what was learned;
- what is entrusted/let go;
- what depends on me tomorrow;
- action toward financial/business goal;
- focus/time loss;
- mood.

The journal is a reflection record, not the main task manager.
Operational items flow into Tasks.

## CEO Review
Weekly business reflection covering money, drivers, waste, high-leverage actions, focus loss, next goal and key money tasks.

## Knowledge Base
Intended flow:
idea/knowledge → why it matters → application → test/status → result → system or discard.

## Capital
Tracks financial position and obligations such as cash/accounts, marketplace payouts, inventory at cost, supplier debt, taxes, upcoming purchases and reserve.

## Tasks
A task is atomic enough to close with a checkbox.
Bad task: “Find wholesale sales channels”.
Better tasks:
- select 30 SKUs for wholesale catalog;
- collect price/stock data;
- finish first catalog page;
- find 20 combat-sports stores;
- collect buyer contacts;
- contact first 10 prospects.

## Projects
A project provides the bigger outcome/context around tasks.
Example:
Project: launch wholesale sales channel.
Outcome: first 5 active wholesale clients.
Tasks: concrete next actions.

## Workout data semantics
Separate:
- program/template = intended exercise prescription;
- scheduled session = workout occurrence on a date;
- session result = actual workout execution;
- sets = actual weight/reps per set.

Moving one scheduled occurrence must not mutate the recurring weekly schedule.
Changing recurring schedule should affect future occurrences, not rewrite history.

## Quran reading
Reading unit is a coherent block targeted at about 5–10 minutes.
- Short surah: may be whole surah.
- Long surah: coherent grouped ayat.
- Do not split purely by arbitrary fixed count if it harms reading context.

For Al-Fatiha, display all seven ayat.
Translation and tafsir are distinct layers.

## Person of the Day
A lightweight discovery/reading service. User wants the pleasure of learning that a person existed and what they contributed, not a forced self-improvement worksheet.
